The hunters who had caught him took him to a place, where he was chained
up among all the other wild beasts. The prince decided to show his sorrow for
his past bad behavior by being gentle with the man tasked to care for him.
Unfortunately, this man, the caretaker, was very tough and mean. Even though
the poor beast did what he was told, the caretaker often hit him for no reason
just because he was in a bad mood.
One day when the caretaker was asleep, a tiger broke off its chain and flew
him, wanting to cat him up. Prince Darling, who saw what was going on, at
first felt quite pleased to think that the mean caretaker deserved this, but upon
reflection, he changed his mind.
