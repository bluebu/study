When Judy was 6 years old, she moved to a new neighborhood in Los Angeles. Judy had always talked in Spanish, at the new school, she wasn't allowed to talk in Spanish. Judy had few Mexican-American classmates. At first, it was difficult for Judy to pronounce and understand English words. Her teacher let Judy draw and paint when she couldn't understand the lessons. Judy began to love painting. Later, she studied modern art in college.
