Shortly after they met, Mr. Sesemann told him, "I want to arrange for Clara
to visit Heidi on the mountain."
After thinking for a while, he continued. "She wants to send some gifts to
Heidi. If she cannot go by herself, would you please go instead of her?" he asked
Dr. Classen.
When the doctor showed up at Heidi's place, Heidi thought Clara also came,
but the doctor explained the reason why she did not.
