Heidi turned to her grandfather and asked, "When can I go outside.
Grandfather?"
Grandfather looked at the deep snow and replied "Not now Heidi The
snow is too deep. But you can go soon."
Day after day, Heidi would ask, "Grandfather, is today the day we can visit
Grannie?"
And then one day, Grandfather smiled at her and said, "Yes, Heidi. Today,
we can go see Grannie." With excitement, they got on their sleds and slid down
the mountain to Grannie's little hut
