As for Heidi, her wish was for Grannie to have a solid bed with warm
coverings. Mr. Seemann saw no difficulty in fulfilling that wish. "The bed will
be sent here in no time," he said.
At first, Grannie thought Heidi was there to say goodbye. "Those people
from Frankfit are going to take her away," she thought bitterly. But after Heidi
told her what was to happen, Grannie breathed a sigh of relief, and a big
turned up on her face.
