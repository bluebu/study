Heidi liked Grandmamma right away. The old lady was kind and she cared
about Heidi. When she realized that Heidi didn't have much to do when Clara
took her afternoon rest, she read books to her. When Heidi begged her to read
more, Grandmamma had an idea.
"Why don't you try reading it yourself? You are smart. I'm sure you'll learn
it in no time," she encouraged Heidi.
That evening, Grandmamma gave Heidi the book with pictures of green
grass and goats as a present.
