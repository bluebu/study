Detie took Heidi to the Sesemanns,
a very wealthy family in Frankfurt. Mr.
Sesemann was often away working, so he
hired Miss Rottenmeier to look after his
wheelchair-bound daughter Clara. Since
the girl's mother died a long time ago,
Mr. Sesemann thought Clara's happiness
was his most important thing.
Miss Rottenmeier liked bossing servants around in the house. She was not
friendly. When she saw Heidi, she asked her about her name and age, but neither
of which satisfied her. "Heidi is not a proper name," and "You are not Clara's
age," were her words. When she learnt that Heidi couldn't read, she became very
surprised. "Good lord! What kind of girl your age doesn't read?" she said.
