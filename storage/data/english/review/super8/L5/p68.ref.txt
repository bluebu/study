As I was walking on the beach, I saw something in the water. It was an
upside-down boat! I waded out to get a better look and found that it was not a
small boat, but a big one. The boat was big enough to hold me. I was excited!
Most English ships have a rowboat on their deck in case of an emergency.
Perhaps a storm had washed this one off a ship. What a wonderful discovery!
This might be a way for me to escape and go back home to my wife and
children.
