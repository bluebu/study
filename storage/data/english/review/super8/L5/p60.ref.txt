There were two political parties in Lilliput, the High-Heels and the Low-
Heels. The two groups hated each other. Once, the High-Heels were the most
popular group in the country. However, the king, the queen, and their followers
belonged to the Low-Heels. To make matters worse, the king's son wore a high
heel on one foot and a low heel on the other foot. So as he walked, he moved
up and down. This upset the king greatly.
I was curious about the war, and Reldresal explained to me that it started
because Lilliput and Blefuscu had a disagreement on how to peel boiled eggs.
The people of Lilliput used to peel their eggs at the big end, but when the king's
grandfather was a little boy, he accidentally cut his finger while peeling an egg
at the big end. As a result, his father declared a new law that in the future,
the people of Lilliput would always peel their eggs at the little end. Those who
obeyed this new law were called Small-Enders, and those who did not were
called Big-Enders.
