The man wearing a blue coat told me that over five hundred thousand tiny
people lived in Lilliput. Soon, I reached the king's palace. It was in the center
of the city and surrounded by a big wall. As I climbed over the wall, I saw into
the palace rooms and found everything was so beautiful. The queen was talking
to other ladies. Upon seeing me, she moved to the window and warmly said,
"Enormous One, welcome to our lovely city. You may kiss my hand."
In the following days, I made my observations of Lilliput. I found that people
who did something good got rewarded, and those who did something bad were
punished. The big surprise was that people who had obeyed the law for six years
could get some money from the king. So in Lilliput, everybody behaved well in
order to get the reward. There were many good people there.
