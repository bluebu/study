A few days later, to my surprise, the king and queen paid me a visit. They
were both larger than most of the Lilliputians and dressed in magnificent clothes.
Though I tried very hard, I could not communicate with the king because he
spoke to me in his own language, which I couldn't understand. The crowds
around us were excited and curious, eager to hear what the king was saying.
What a pity! Our language barrier made it impossible for us to understand each
other. Eventually, the king and queen left, leaving the guards to watch over me
and keep the crowds calm.
