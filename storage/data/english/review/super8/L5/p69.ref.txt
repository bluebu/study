The next day, the Blefuscan king received a letter from the king of Lilliput,
who wanted me back under guard. He declared that I was a criminal and
intended to blind me as punishment.
I explained to the Blefuscan king that the advisers in Lilliput were evil and
told lies about me. The kind Blefuscan king didn't want me to suffer such a cruel
punishment. He said, "It's time for you to go back to your own country. I will
tell the king of Lilliput that you are leaving Blefuscu."
