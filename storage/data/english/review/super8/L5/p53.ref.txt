I looked down and couldn't believe my eyes. There was a tiny man standing
on my chest, not bigger than my thumb! He had a bow and arrows in his hands.
There were many more tiny men crawling on me. I was so surprised that I
yelled, "Get off me, you bugs!"
The tiny men were so scared that they jumped and screamed. Some of them
fell off me. I tried to move, but I was tied up with many thin ropes, like string. I
tried hard to get free, and finally, my left arm was loose.
