The promises were:
1. I had to help the country in war and peace.
2. Before I could visit the capital, I had to give a two-hour warning so the
little people could get out of the street.
3. I could not step on the little people or the animals.
4. I had to carry important messages from the king to others in Lilliput.
5. I had to help workers carry heavy objects.
6. I could not leave the kingdom without getting permission.
