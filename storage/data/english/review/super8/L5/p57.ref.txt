Thankfully, I had a smart idea. I took out my handkerchief and tied cach
corner to a strong stick. Then, I planted the four sticks firmly into the ground.
With this new setup, if any of the men fell, they would land safely on the
handkerchief below the rope. It made the sport much safer, and the king praised
me for my cleverness. He even called it a marvelous invention. Everyone was
amazed and thought I was wonderful!
As time went on, I continued to ask the king to set me free. Finally, he said, "l
have talked to my advisers about you. We agree that you have been good to our
people. I will set you free if you make six promises."
