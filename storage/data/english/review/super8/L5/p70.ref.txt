Seven months later, I finally arrived home, where my family welcomed me
with tears of joy. They were fascinated by my stories, while others thought I was
crazy or making up tales about the little people I met.
To prove their existence, I showed them the tiny Blefuscan animals, and
then everyone believed my extraordinary journey.
Months passed, and while I enjoyed being with my family, I started to feel
bored. England was too small and familiar, and I longed for more exciting
adventures and unknown places to explore.
