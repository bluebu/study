"Do you think," the Cowardly Lion asked, "if I go to the Emerald City with
you, that Oz would give me courage?"
They soon heard strange noises in the depths of the forest, and the Lion
whispered to them that the Kalidahs lived there.
"What are the Kalidahs?" asked the girl, Dorothy.
"They are huge beasts with bodies like bears and heads like tigers," replied
the Lion, "and with claws so long and sharp that they could tear me in two as
easily as I could kill Toto. I'm terribly afraid of the Kalidahs."
