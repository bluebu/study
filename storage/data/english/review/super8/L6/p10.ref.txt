"I'm the Witch of the North," replied the woman. "But I'm a kind-hearted
witch, beloved by the people. If I had the same power as the wicked witch who
once ruled here, I would have freed the Munchkins myself."
"But I thought all witches and wizards were wicked," remarked Dorothy.
"Oh no,"
the woman explained. "In the Land of Oz, we only had four
witches. Two are nice, living in the north and the south. I'm one of the nice
ones. The other two witches from the east and the west were mean. But you
defeated the mean one from the east, so now we only have one mean witch left,
in the west. And we also have a great wizard named Oz. He's a good wizard,
the strongest of all, and lives in the Emerald City."
