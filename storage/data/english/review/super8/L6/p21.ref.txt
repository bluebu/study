Dorothy asked about his magic, and Oz explained that he was just a
common man. The Scarecrow asked for brains, but Oz replied that he didn't
need them as he was constantly learning and getting smarter. The Lion said he
needed courage, but Oz told him he was already brave, just needed to believe in
himself. The Tin Woodman wanted a heart, but Oz said having a heart could
make him sad sometimes, and the Woodman was lucky.
Dorothy then asked how she could return to Kansas. Oz said that he had no
idea and suggested she call the Winged Monkeys to carry her across the desert.
