With Toto following closely, she began her journey. It did not take her long
to find the road paved with yellow bricks. Within a short time, she was walking
briskly toward the Emerald City. By evening, Dorothy arrived at the home of
one of the richest Munchkins in the land, whose name was Boq. Boq led her
into the house, where he gave her a room with a pretty bed in it. Toto curled
up on the blue rug beside her.
