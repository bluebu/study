Glinda then turned to the Scarecrow and asked about his plans once Dorothy
had left. The Scarecrow revealed his intention to return to the Emerald City,
where he was well-liked by the people. His only concern was how to cross the hill.
Next, Glinda asked the Tin Woodman what he planned to do after Dorothy
left. The Tin Woodman said he wanted to go back to the Winkies' country,
where they liked him and wanted him to be their ruler. Glinda promised to ask
the Winged Monkeys to take him safely to the land of the Winkies.
Then, Glinda asked the Lion what he would do when Dorothy went home.
The Lion said he wanted to go back to the big forest, where the animals there
treated him like a king. Glinda promised to ask the Winged Monkeys to take
him back to his favorite forest.
