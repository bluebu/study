"I am the Tin Woodman," answered the tin man. "How did you happen to
be here?"
"We are on our way to the Emerald City to see the Great Oz," she answered.
"Why do you wish to see Oz?" he asked.
"I want him to send me back to Kansas, and the Scarecrow wants him to
put a few brains into his head," she replied.
The Tin Woodman appeared to think deeply for a moment. Then he said,
"Do you think if I go to the Emerald City with you, that Oz would give me a
heart?"
