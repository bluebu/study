Hiding her true intentions, the witch secretly placed an iron bar on the
kitchen floor, unseen by anyone. As Dorothy crossed the floor, she stumbled over
the hidden bar and lost one of her silver shoes. The Wicked Witch was pleased
with her successful trick, now that she had one of the shoes in her possession.
Dorothy was very upset about losing
her shoe and asked for it back. The
witch, refusing to give it back, claimed it
as her own. Dorothy's anger grew, and
she grabbed a nearby bucket of water,
soaking the witch from head to foot.
To Dorothy's astonishment, the wicked
woman let out a cry of fear and began to shrink and dissolve. In a short time,
she had melted away completely.
