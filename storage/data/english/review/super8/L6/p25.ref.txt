Aunt Em had just stepped out of the house to tend to the cabbages when she
glanced up and saw Dorothy rushing toward her. Overwhelmed with joy, she
hugged the little girl tightly, showering her with kisses. "My dear child! Where
on earth did you come from?" she exclaimed.
Dorothy, with a serious expression, replied, "I've come from the Land of Oz,
Aunt Em. And look, Toto is here too! Oh, Aunt Em, I'm so glad to be home!"
