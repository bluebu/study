On this particular day, Uncle Henry sat on the doorstep, his gaze fixed
anxiously upon the unusually gray sky. Aunt Em was busy washing the dishes.
Suddenly, Uncle Henry stood up and called out to his wife, "Em, there's a
cyclone coming! I'll go check on the livestock." With that, he hurried toward the
sheds where the cows and horses were kept.
Aunt Em stopped her work and rushed to the door. A single glance showed
there was danger. "Quick, Dorothy!" she screamed. "Run to the cellar!" Dorothy
managed to grab Toto and followed her aunt. However, halfway across the
room, a fierce gust of wind roared, causing the house to tremble violently.
Losing her balance, Dorothy tumbled to the floor.
