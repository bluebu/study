"Wait a minute!" called the Scarecrow. He had been thinking about what
was best to be done, and now he asked the Woodman to chop away the end of
the tree that rested on their side of the ditch. The Tin Woodman began to use
his axe at once, and just as the two Kalidahs were nearly across,
the tree fell
with a crash into the gulf. The falling branch carried the ugly, snarling brutes
with it. Both were dashed to pieces on the sharp rocks at the bottom. It was not
long before they reached the road of yellow bricks and turned again toward the
Emerald City, where the Great Oz lived.
