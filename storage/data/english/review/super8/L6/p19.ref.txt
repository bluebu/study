In her cupboard, the Wicked Witch kept a golden cap adorned with
diamonds and rubies. This cap possessed a special charm. By wearing it, the
owner could summon the Winged Monkeys and give them three commands.
However, the cap's power could only be used three times in total. The Wicked
Witch had already used it twice, so she hesitated to use it again until she had
used all her other means.
