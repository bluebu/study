After a while, Dorothy and her
friends started for the Throne Room of
the Great Oz. They opened a little door,
and what interested Dorothy most was
the big throne of green marble that stood
in the middle of the room. It was shaped
like a chair. In the center of the chair was
an enormous head, without a body to support it or any arms or legs whatsoever.
