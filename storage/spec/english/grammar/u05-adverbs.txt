[三] 从方框中选择最恰当的副词，并用其正确形式填空，每个词只能用一次。 | 10 题
词库: often, why, away, politely, again, outside, too, how, early, always, suddenly
例: I *often* go to school by bus. | 我经常乘公共汽车上学。
Tom speaks __ to his teacher. | politely
Can you wait for me if you arrive there __ than I? | earlier
The railway station is a few miles __. | away
John is __ late for school. This makes his teacher very angry. | always
–__ did you throw away the cake? | Why
    –It tastes sour.
The children are playing games __ in the sun. | outside
Mom is __ happy to say a word. | too
–__ do you like the dishes? | How
    –Very much.
The driver braked (刹车) __ when he saw a dog ahead (在前方). | suddenly
What bad weather! It's raining __. | again

[五] 根据中文提示完成句子。 | 12 题
例: Mary runs *as* *fast* *as* I do. | 玛丽跑得和我一样快。
Mary jumps __ I do. | as high as
    玛丽跳得和我一样高。
I write __ Sarah does. | as neatly as
    我写得和萨拉一样工整。
Julie works __ Tom. | harder than
    朱丽比汤姆学习刻苦。
Helen listens to the teacher __ John. | more carefully than
    海伦听讲比约翰认真。
I do __ Jim does. | not skate as well as
    我溜冰没有吉姆溜得好。
Sarah __ I do. | doesn't read as many books as
    萨拉读的书没有我读的多。
Which one do you like __ all the three? | best of
    这三个之中你最喜欢哪一个？
Lucy sings __. | best in our class
    露西在我们班唱歌最好。
Henry loves birds __ I do. | more than
    亨利比我更喜欢鸟。
Let's meet __. | at seven thirty on Sunday morning
    咱们星期日早上七点半集合。
My sister gets up __ I. | earlier than
    姐姐比我起得早。
Tom plays basketball __ Jim. | as well as
    汤姆篮球打得和吉姆一样好。
